## Questions

**Is Caretta free?**\
Yes. The software and the simulation are free and open source (Apache 2.0). Only the kit will cost money.

**Is all the hardware open source?**\
Yes. The whole robot is open source, including the design files and the parts list, so you can build your own.

**Can I use Caretta in my class or lab?**\
Yes. Start in the simulation with no hardware, then move on to the real robot. I'd love to hear how you use it.
